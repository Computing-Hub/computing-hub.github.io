How I teach

Find the gap first

Every new student starts with a short diagnostic. Most struggles trace back to two or three missing ideas, not to the whole subject.

One idea at a time

Visual models, worked examples and small steps. I build interactive explainers for exactly this, and we use them in sessions.

Real understanding, not memorising

Students should be able to explain why, and apply the idea to a question they have never seen. That is what the exams reward.

Write code, don't just read it

Computer Science is practical. We write and debug real Python together, in the browser, from the first session.

Exam technique from an examiner

Command words, mark allocation, how to show working, and what loses marks needlessly.

Inclusive by default

Calm layouts, read-aloud, easy-read and one-step-at-a-time options are built into my resources, and I adapt sessions for neurodivergent learners.
