About

About BeanWise

BeanWise is a Jersey-based tuition service from Coderra.je Limited, teaching Computer Science from KS3 to A-level and Maths from KS3 to GCSE. Lessons are one-to-one or in small groups, online or face to face, and every student works with the same interactive resources and the same structured approach.

How lessons work

Find the gap first

Every new student starts with a short diagnostic. Most struggles trace back to two or three missing ideas, not to the whole subject.

One idea at a time

Visual models, worked examples and small steps, supported by interactive explainers built for exactly this and used in sessions.

Real understanding, not memorising

Students should be able to explain why, and apply the idea to a question they have never seen. That is what the exams reward.

Write code, don't just read it

Computer Science is practical. Students write and debug real Python in the browser from the first session.

Examiner-informed exam technique

Command words, mark allocation, how to show working, and what loses marks needlessly.

Inclusive by default

Calm layouts, read-aloud, easy-read and one-step-at-a-time options are built into the resources, and sessions are adapted for neurodivergent learners.

More than the lesson

A shared resource library

Interactive activities, Parsons puzzles, block-based coding tools and visual explainers, free to use between lessons. Browse the resources.

Members area

Tuition students can log in for members-only exam practice, course plans and a dashboard that tracks progress against the specification.

Clear records for parents

Session notes after every lesson, and progress tracked topic by topic, so families can see what is moving.

What we can cover

Computer Science

  • Systems architecture, memory and storage
  • Data representation: binary, hex, characters, images, sound
  • Networks, protocols and security
  • Systems software and ethical, legal and environmental issues
  • Algorithms: searching, sorting, trace tables, Big O
  • Python programming, pseudocode and exam reference language
  • Boolean logic, databases and SQL
  • A-level: OOP, data structures, computational methods, the NEA project

Maths

  • Number: fractions, percentages, ratio, standard form, bounds
  • Algebra: expressions, equations, sequences, graphs, quadratics
  • Geometry: angles, Pythagoras, trigonometry, circle theorems
  • Probability and statistics
  • Problem-solving and multi-step questions
  • Route to a 4: securing the Foundation core before pushing higher
  • KS3 foundations for students who have fallen behind

Who teaches

Lessons are taught by a Computer Science teacher with fourteen years in secondary classrooms, currently teaching in Jersey. Background: a PhD in Computer Science from Queen Mary University of London, marking OCR GCSE Computer Science (J277) papers every summer since 2019, and several years in IT consultancy before teaching. Lessons can be given in English, Spanish or French.
