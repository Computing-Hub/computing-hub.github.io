Route to a 4

These are the topics that earn a grade 4 in Maths. Work through them in order. Tick a topic when you can do it without help. Your ticks are saved on this device only.

Stage 1 · Strong foundations

Get these solid first. Everything else is built on them.

Number
  • Negative numbers ▶ video ready
  • Order of operations (BIDMAS)
  • Rounding and estimation
  • Fractions, decimals and percentages ▶ videos: fractions
  • Fraction arithmetic (+ − × ÷) ▶ videos: fractions
  • Prime factors, HCF and LCM ▶ videos: HCF, LCM
  • Powers and roots
Algebra
  • Algebra notation (3x means 3 times x)
  • Substitution
  • Coordinates

Stage 2 · The grade 4 core

These topics carry most of the grade 4 marks on the paper.

Number
  • Percentage change ▶ video ready
  • Simple interest and money
  • Standard form
  • Index laws
Ratio and proportion
  • Dividing in a ratio
  • Direct and inverse proportion
  • Scale factors and maps
  • Money and best buys
  • Speed and density (compound measures)
Algebra
  • Solving linear equations
  • Expanding and factorising
  • Sequences and the nth term
  • Straight-line graphs
  • Rearranging formulae
Geometry
  • Angle facts
  • Perimeter, area and volume
  • Circles
  • Units and conversions (incl. time)
  • Transformations
Statistics and probability
  • Averages and range
  • Charts and graphs
  • Frequency tables
  • The probability scale
  • Sample spaces and Venn diagrams

Stage 3 · Push to a 5

Secure on Stages 1 and 2? These take you past the pass line.

  • Pythagoras' theorem
  • Simultaneous equations
  • Quadratic graphs
  • Right-angled trigonometry
  • Compound interest
  • Relative frequency
  • Tree diagrams
