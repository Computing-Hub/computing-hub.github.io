Computer science resources, all in one place

Computing Hub brings together a growing collection of educational projects for teaching and learning computer science. Each project lives in its own folder in this site and can be reached from the navigation above.

You'll find diagnostic questions to uncover misconceptions, short explainer videos on key topics, and hands-on GCSE activities, with more being added over time.
