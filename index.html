How it works

No big upfront blocks. We start small, find out what is actually needed, and you pay session by session.

Free 15-minute call

A short conversation with you and your child about where things are, what the target is and when the exams are.

Baseline check

A short, exam-board-neutral test done at home. It shows me the gaps, and shows us both whether the student is ready to put the work in.

A plan, not a sales pitch

I recommend how many sessions and what we cover. If a few sessions will do it, I will say so.

Pay per session

Card or QR payment via SumUp, or bank transfer for ongoing students. An invoice for every lesson, no contracts.

Why families choose SmartBeans
