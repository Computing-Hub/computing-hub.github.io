How booking and payment work

Free intro call

Fifteen minutes online to talk about where the student is and what the goal is. No charge, no obligation.

Baseline check

A short exam-board-neutral test taken at home. I use it to see the gaps and recommend how many sessions are worth booking.

Book sessions

Choose 45- or 60-minute slots online through the booking calendar. Reschedule or cancel free with 24 hours' notice.

Pay per lesson

You receive an invoice for each lesson. Pay by card or QR code through SumUp, or by bank transfer using the invoice number as the reference.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a session?

Use the booking calendar to pick a 45- or 60-minute slot, or send a message and I will suggest times. New students start with the free intro call.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Reschedule or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Lessons cancelled with less notice are charged.

Online or face-to-face?

Both, at the same price. Online sessions use Google Meet or Zoom. Face-to-face sessions are available at agreed locations in Jersey; please get in touch for details.

How do group sessions work?

Bring one or two friends who are studying the same subject at a similar level, ideally for the same exam board. One person books and the group splits the cost however they like.

Why no bundles or blocks?

I would rather you pay for what is actually needed. After the baseline check I recommend a number of sessions; if a handful will do it, that is what I will say. Ongoing students usually pay by bank transfer after each lesson.

What if the student is not engaging?

Tuition only works when the student puts the time in. The baseline check is partly a commitment check, and if it is not working I will tell you rather than keep taking bookings.

Which exam boards?

Computer Science: OCR J277 and H446, Edexcel 1CP2, AQA 8525 and 7517. Maths: any GCSE board, Foundation or Higher, and KS3.
